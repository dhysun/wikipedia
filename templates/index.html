About

Wikipedia Pathfinder utilizes a bidirectional Breadth-First-Search to find the shortest possible path between any two Wikipedia articles. Simply enter the starting and target article, and click search! The website is up to date as of June 1, 2026. Finally, feel free to check out our GitHub! It contains more in-depth details on how the website was built.

Credits to the Wikipedia Speedrunning Community and Six Degrees of Wikipedia for inspiring this project!
